# 15 — Chad Deterministic Decision Loop

Goal: compose existing Family OS laws into one inspectable next-step decision without performing external writes.

Order:
1. Recoverable unknown → RECOVER before human interruption.
2. Classify Digital / Physical / Together from explicit work signals.
3. Resolve authority only for Chad-executed digital action.
4. Physical reality can add watch/friction but proven tight fit stays usable.
5. Human authority/judgment blocking the goal → minimal ASK.
6. Authorized digital-only work → EXECUTE.
7. Physical or mixed work → HANDOFF/Together path; completion still needs readback.

The loop returns reasons and states; it does not mutate Calendar, Gmail, JDK, or Maps.
